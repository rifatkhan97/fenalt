### B. Trade, Tariff & Customs Audit
- **Audit Result**: CORRECTIONS APPLIED.
- **Finding**: Absolute phrases like "duty-free tariff treatment" were identified on trade pages.
- **Resolution**:
  - Replaced absolute wording with qualified, conditional language: *"may qualify for preferential tariff treatment subject to applicable rules of origin and customs entry requirements."*
  - Added formal customs disclaimers to all trade guides:
    > *Disclaimer: Trade regulations, tariff rates, and customs rules vary by product category, material composition, and jurisdiction, and are subject to change. Importers should verify specific tariff classifications and duty requirements with a licensed customs broker or relevant trade authority.*

### C. Timelines & MOQs Audit
- **Audit Result**: CORRECTIONS APPLIED.
- **Finding**: Timelines (10–14 day sampling, 4–6 week production) needed explicit framing as estimates dependent on fabric sourcing and tech pack readiness.
- **Resolution**: Updated copy across commercial routes to clarify sampling and production timelines as realistic estimates rather than unconditional guarantees.

### D. Product Specifications Audit
- **Audit Result**: CORRECTIONS APPLIED.
- **Finding**: Specific fabric weights (e.g. 380gsm–480gsm fleece, 240gsm–300gsm tees, 12GG/14GG knits) were audited to ensure they are presented as available options rather than mandatory specs for every order.
- **Resolution**: Added clarifying copy: *"Available specifications include..."* and *"Custom weights available upon request."*

### E. Industry Statistics Audit
